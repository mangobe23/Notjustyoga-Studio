Why Yin Yoga Complements a Rehab Plan 為什麼陰瑜伽是復健計劃的最佳夥伴

A short look at how slow, held postures support tissue healing. 簡短談談緩慢的靜態體位如何幫助組織修復。

Yin yoga asks you to hold a small number of postures for several minutes at a time, well past the point where the muscles have relaxed. This is deliberate — the goal isn't the muscle at all, but the connective tissue around the joints. 陰瑜伽要求你將少數幾個體位維持數分鐘之久，遠遠超過肌肉放鬆所需的時間。這是刻意的設計——目標並非肌肉本身，而是關節周圍的結締組織。
