Physiotherapy 物理治療

Clinical care, studio warmth 臨床專業，工作室的溫度

Assessment, hands-on treatment and strength work — for injuries from sport, from the mat, or from everyday life. 評估、徒手治療與肌力訓練——無論傷痛來自運動、瑜伽墊，還是日常生活。

01

Sports Injury Assessment運動傷害診斷

Initial consultation 初次諮詢 45–60 min

A thorough assessment to understand what's actually going on — the movement patterns behind the pain, not just the symptom — and a clear plan going forward. 全面評估疼痛背後真正的原因與動作模式，而不只是處理表面症狀，並提供清楚的後續治療計劃。

Book a consultation 預約諮詢
02

Rehabilitation復健

Ongoing programme 階段性療程 45 min / session

A staged recovery programme built around your injury and your goals — whether that's returning to sport, to your yoga practice, or to pain-free daily movement. 依照你的傷勢與目標設計的階段性復健計劃——無論目標是重返運動場、回到瑜伽墊，或只是無痛地完成日常動作。

Book a consultation 預約諮詢
03

Manual Therapy徒手治療

Hands-on treatment 徒手療程 30–45 min

Joint mobilisation, soft tissue release and other hands-on techniques to ease pain, restore range of motion and support the rest of your programme. 關節鬆動術、軟組織放鬆等徒手技巧，幫助緩解疼痛、恢復關節活動度，並支持整體治療計劃。

Book a consultation 預約諮詢
04

Strength Training肌力訓練

1-on-1 一對一 45–60 min

Targeted strength work to build resilience once the acute pain has settled — so the injury doesn't simply come back. 在急性疼痛緩解後，透過針對性的肌力訓練建立身體韌性，避免傷害再次復發。
