About our team- Physiotherapist

專業團隊介紹

Brett Liu is a physiotherapist with over 10 years of experience across elite sport, private practice, and healthcare in Sydney and London.

As Head Physiotherapist for a professional Australian Football Club, Brett developed deep expertise in sports training, injury rehabilitation, and strength rebuilding.

In London, Brett has taken on leadership and teaching roles, including Senior Physiotherapist at Bupa and Lecturer at Brunel University, with a focus on functional training for older adults.

Holding an Executive MBA in Healthcare Operations and Management, Brett combines clinical expertise with strategic insight to deliver outstanding, personalised care.

Brett Liu 擁有超過10年物理治療經驗，足跡橫跨雪梨與倫敦的頂尖運動、私人診所與醫療機構。

曾任雪梨職業澳式足球俱樂部首席物理治療師，專精運動訓練、運動傷害復健與肌力重建。

在倫敦擔任多項領導與教學職務，包括 Bupa 資深物理治療師，醫療診所運營總監及 Brunel University 講師，專注於老年人功能性訓練。

擁有醫療運營管理 Executive MBA 學位，結合臨床專業與策略思維，提供卓越且個人化的治療服務。
