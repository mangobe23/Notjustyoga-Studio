Courses 課程

Find your practice 找到屬於你的練習

From slow, restorative Yin to focused 1-on-1 work, every course is taught in small groups so we can actually see how you move. 從緩慢修復的陰瑜伽到專注的一對一課程，我們維持小班教學，才能真正看見你身體的每個動作。

01

Hatha Yoga哈達瑜伽

All levels 程度不限 Group class 團體課 60 min

Steady, foundational postures held with attention to alignment and breath. A grounding class for building strength and body awareness at a measured pace. 穩定而扎根的基礎體位法，著重於身體排列與呼吸的覺察。以穩健的步調建立力量與身體覺知的一堂課。

Enquire to book 洽詢預約
02

Yin Yoga陰瑜伽

All levels 程度不限 Group class 團體課 75 min

Long-held, passive postures that target the connective tissue. A quiet, slow-paced practice — often recommended alongside a physiotherapy plan. 長時間停留的靜態伸展，作用於結締組織。安靜且緩慢的練習，經常搭配物理治療計劃一起進行。

Enquire to book 洽詢預約
03

1-on-1 Private Session一對一私人課

Personalised 客製化 In-studio or online 工作室或線上 60 min

One-to-one time to work on a specific goal, an old injury, or simply to build a home practice with proper feedback. 針對特定目標、舊傷或想建立居家練習習慣，提供一對一指導與即時回饋。

Enquire to book 洽詢預約
04

Outdoor Park Yoga — London戶外公園瑜伽（倫敦）

Occasional event 不定期活動 All levels 程度不限 Weather dependent 視天氣而定

A few times a year we take the mats outside to one of London's parks. Dates are announced on our socials and here — join the mailing list via the contact form to hear about the next one. 我們每年會安排幾次，把瑜伽墊帶到倫敦的公園裡進行戶外練習。活動日期會公告於我們的社群與此頁面——歡迎透過聯絡表單加入通知名單。
